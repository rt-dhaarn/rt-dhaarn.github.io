Joint Superposition Collapse • thrml

Thermodynamic Jam Collapse

Holding conflicting fleet trajectories in superposition and collapsing them into a single coherent joint plan via block Gibbs sampling on discrete energy-based models.

01. Where Jams Come From

Each FLOWRRA fleet reads a local density field in a Gibbs frame, $P \propto \exp(-E/T)$, with the floor's throughput pressure (orders left against time left) acting as temperature $T$. A shared GNN scores moves and a rule ladder resolves conflicts.

The Gap: In a tight jam, deciding fleet-by-fleet causes agents to yield and advance in turn repeatedly. FLOWRRA's founding design calls for a joint collapse: sampling a consistent multi-agent plan from an Energy-Based Model using Extropic's thrml library ahead of sampling hardware.

02. Categorical EBM Formulation

  • Boundaries: Jams are conflicting clusters within 3 hops (plus standing congestion zones). Outside fleets, people, and debris stay clamped to recorded positions.
  • Variables: One categorical variable per fleet per tick restricted to its spacetime light cone (reachable cells that still reach the exit in time, plus "out").
  • Pairwise Energies: Move at most 1 hop/tick; no shared cells; no edge/cell swaps; cost of $1$ per tick not yet out.

03. Block Gibbs Annealing

  • Architecture: CategoricalEBMFactor & CategoricalGibbsConditional partitioned into even-tick and odd-tick blocks.
  • Schedule: 32 parallel chains annealing from $\beta = 0.1 \to 10$. Half initialize from shortest paths; half relax rules with finite penalties while hot to escape tight spots.
  • Verification: Graded against exact $A^*$ search on jams up to 4 fleets.
